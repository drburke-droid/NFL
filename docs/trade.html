How it works

Player level. Each player's rest-of-season points per game come from a per-position ridge regression fitted on every NFL season since 2012: this season (average, last four, best game, games played), the two prior seasons, the career, and the FantasyFootballAnalytics consensus projection for the coming week. Walk-forward it misses by 3.1 points per game against 3.3 for "this year blended with last", and it does not believe two-week hot starts the way that blend does.

Your scoring. The model is fitted in one league's scoring, so each player is carried as a per-game stat line (the projection's mix of yards, catches and touchdowns, scaled to the fitted level) and re-scored in yours: a quarterback in a 4-point-TD league, a tight end with a reception premium.

Lineups, not trade charts. A trade is worth what it does to the best lineup each team can field each remaining week: bye weeks out, injured players out as often as their status says (Questionable players miss about a third of the rest of the season, Out about half), and any slot the bench cannot cover filled by a generally available free agent (the average of the best five unrostered at the position, at his full rate, since you pick up someone who is playing that week), and the worst player cut when a team takes back more bodies than it sends. 200 simulated seasons per trade.

Wins. Weekly points become win probability against a league-average opponent, including each lineup's week-to-week spread (which grows with the player: a 22-point player swings by about 9, a 4-point one by 4). That is why a strong team gains less from the same points than a middling one.

Sell high / buy low tags. Early-season points split into what a player's volume (targets, carries, attempts) earns and the rest. Over 2016-25 the rest mostly disappears: the luckiest fifth fell 4.8 points per game after week 3, the unluckiest rose 2.9. Shown as a tag; the level already regresses.

Limits. This season only (no keeper or dynasty value); kickers and defenses are treated as interchangeable; scoring rules beyond yards, touchdowns, catches, turnovers, reception premiums and 100/300-yard bonuses are ignored and listed above.
