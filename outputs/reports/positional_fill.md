## Opportunity cost of paying up (tier-1 PPG vs realistic fill)

Cost of NOT paying = (tier-1 season pts) - (expected fill season pts over the same 13 ROS weeks
+ startable early weeks). Using median-3rd-best discoverable fill (competition-adjusted).

```
pos  tier-1 PPG (elite bar) fill PPG (waiver 3rd)  PPG gap season pts gap
QB                    25.1                  17.2      7.9            103
RB                    18.4                   6.3     12.1            157
WR                    18.0                   9.7      8.4            109
TE                    14.8                   7.3      7.5             98
```

Interpretation: season points you CANNOT recover from the wire if you skip tier-1 at that position.
Higher = position deserves your auction dollars; lower = fill it cheap and reallocate.
